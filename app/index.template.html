VT17 Test Generator

From Excel test scenarios to test XBRL files based on the Dutch real estate taxonomy (VT17 or VT15, chosen per row). Anything you leave out is filled in with realistic data.

1

Fill in the template

One row per test. Each row becomes one XBRL file.

Per test you fill in
  • Taxonomy (VT17 or VT15) and data product
  • Case ID and valuer report ID
  • Use: commercial, residential, mixed or agricultural
  • Address of the property
  • One or more VBO IDs, each with its use and market value
  • Valuation date and the valuer's CoC number

The rest of the XBRL is filled with realistic data.

Generate the XBRL files

Each XBRL file is saved straight to your Downloads folder, ready to open.
